Privacy

Your Funbridge sign-in is read from the Funbridge tab's own requests and kept only in Chrome's memory, never on disk; it is forgotten when Chrome closes. The Google token stays in memory only. On disk, Chrome keeps just these settings and the IDs of tournaments already saved. Nothing is sent anywhere except Funbridge, your Downloads folder and, if you turn it on, your Google Drive.
