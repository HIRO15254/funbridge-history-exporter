Export Funbridge history as PBN

Connects to your signed-in Funbridge tab and saves your BP Circuit, Series, and Daily history as PBN 2.1 files.

Not connected

Open a Funbridge tab and click "Detect traffic".

  1. In a tab signed in to Funbridge Web, click "Detect traffic"
  2. In Funbridge, open your history or a board to trigger an API request
  3. Click "Save all history" once it is enabled

The authorization header is kept in memory only and is never written to the PBN files or Chrome storage. Data is sent nowhere except Funbridge and your local download folder.
